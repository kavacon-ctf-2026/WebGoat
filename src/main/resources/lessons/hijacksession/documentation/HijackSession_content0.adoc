This fixed lesson issues an opaque, cryptographically random `hijack_cookie` for your signed-in
WebGoat account. The server checks that the cookie is active and belongs to that account; guessing
another cookie does not authenticate you as its owner.

Create your lesson session. On the first submission, the server issues a fresh cookie; submit the
form again to verify that cookie. Compare it with a new session. The identifiers should have no
sequence or timestamp pattern. Explain why an invalid, expired, or copied identifier does not grant
access to another user's session.
